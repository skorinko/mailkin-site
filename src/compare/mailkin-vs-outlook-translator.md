<p class="answer">If you read your email in Outlook for Mac, Microsoft's Translator is already there: select a message, choose Translator, and read it in your language, translated online by Microsoft. If you read your email in Apple Mail, Mailkin translates incoming emails automatically as you open them, in place, on your Mac. The main question is which mail app you use.</p>

## How Outlook's Translator works on Mac

Microsoft's Mac instructions apply to Outlook for Microsoft 365 for Mac and Outlook 2024 for Mac. You install the Translator for Outlook add-in from Tools > Get Add-ins, then select a received message, choose Translator on the Home tab and pick the language to translate into.

Microsoft says translations are powered by the online Microsoft Translator service, processed in memory on its servers and not stored. On Windows and the web, Microsoft also documents automatic translation, an "ask me before translating" prompt and a "never translate" option, and notes that automatic translation is only available for Exchange Online mailboxes. Its Mac page doesn't describe those settings, so check your own version of Outlook.

## How Mailkin works in Apple Mail

Mailkin is a Mail extension. When an email arrives in one of your 25 supported languages and you open it, it appears already translated in place, keeping its layout, links and images. A banner shows the source language, and Show Original is one click away. You can set each language to automatic, only when you ask, or never, always or never translate a particular sender or email, and correct the detected language. Translation uses Apple's on-device Translation framework, so email content never leaves your Mac, and there is no account.

Mailkin doesn't translate emails you write yet.

## When Outlook's Translator is the better choice

- Outlook for Mac is your mail app, and you don't plan to switch.
- You also use Outlook on Windows or the web and want the same tool everywhere.
- Your organization manages Outlook and its add-ins for you.

## When Mailkin is the better choice

- You read your email in Apple Mail, with any mix of accounts: iCloud, Gmail, Microsoft 365, Exchange or others.
- You want emails to open already translated, keeping their formatting.
- You want per-sender and per-language rules.
- You prefer that email content never leaves your Mac.
