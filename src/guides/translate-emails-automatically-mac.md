<p class="answer">To translate emails automatically on a Mac, use a mail app with built-in translation or add it to the one you use. Apple Mail gets automatic, on-device translation with the Mailkin extension. Outlook for Mac and Gmail in a browser have their own online translation features.</p>

## Apple Mail: add Mailkin

Apple Mail has no automatic translation of its own; it only offers the Translate command on text you select ([details](/guides/translate-email-apple-mail-mac/)). Mailkin adds it:

1. [Download Mailkin](/download), open it and choose the language you want to read email in.
2. Pick the languages you receive email in. macOS downloads each one once.
3. Turn on Mailkin in **Mail › Settings › Extensions**.

From then on, emails in those languages open translated, in place, in all your Mail accounts. Emails already in your language are left alone. For each language you can choose to translate automatically, only when you ask, or never.

**Privacy:** translation happens on your Mac with Apple's built-in translation. Nothing is sent to a server, and there is no account.

## Outlook for Mac

Outlook for Mac includes Microsoft Translator. It can offer to translate a message that isn't in your language, or translate such messages automatically, depending on its translation setting. The translation is made by Microsoft's online service.

**Good for:** people already using Outlook with a Microsoft 365 account. **Consider:** message text is sent to Microsoft to be translated.

## Gmail in the browser

Gmail on the web shows a **Translate message** option for emails in another language, and can be set to always translate a language. The translation is done by Google.

**Good for:** people who read mail in Gmail's web interface. **Consider:** it only works in the browser, not in Apple Mail, and Google translates the content.

## Which should you choose?

If you're happy in Apple Mail and want your email to stay on your Mac, add [Mailkin](/translate-email-apple-mail/). If you need a language outside Apple's [25 on-device languages](/languages/), the online translators of Outlook and Gmail cover more.
