<p class="answer">To translate an email in Apple Mail on a Mac, select the text, Control-click it and choose <strong>Translate</strong>. That translates one selection at a time in a pop-up. To see whole emails translated automatically, in place, add a Mail extension such as Mailkin.</p>

Here are the three ways, from quickest to most complete.

## 1. Use the Translate command in macOS

macOS has translation built in. It works in Mail, as in most apps, on text you select:

1. Open the email in Mail.
2. Select the text you want to translate. Press <kbd>⌘A</kbd> inside the message to select all of it.
3. Control-click (or right-click) the selection and choose **Translate "…"**.
4. A pop-up shows the translation. Choose the languages at the top if macOS guessed wrong, or click **Copy Translation**.

**Good for:** the occasional email. **Limits:** you repeat it for every email, the translation disappears when you close the pop-up, and links, tables and formatting aren't kept. Download languages in **System Settings › General › Language & Region › Translation Languages** to use it offline.

## 2. Copy and paste into a translator

You can also paste the text into the Translate app, a website such as DeepL or Google Translate, or a chatbot.

**Good for:** long documents, or when you want to compare translations. **Limits:** it's the slowest way, and with a website the email's content is sent to that service. Think twice before pasting contracts, invoices or anything personal.

## 3. Translate every email automatically with Mailkin

[Mailkin](/translate-email-apple-mail/) is a Mail extension that does the translating for you. When you open an email in one of the languages you've chosen, Mail shows it already translated, with the layout, links and images intact, and a banner saying which language it was in.

1. [Download Mailkin](/download) and choose the language you read email in.
2. Pick the languages you get email in. macOS downloads each one once from Apple.
3. Turn on Mailkin in **Mail › Settings › Extensions** ([how to turn on Mail extensions](/guides/enable-mail-extensions-mac/)).

Translation runs on your Mac with Apple's built-in translation, so your emails are never sent anywhere. **Show Original** brings back the original text, and you can choose to always or never translate a sender. Your first 30 translated emails are free, then it's a one-time purchase of €14.99 / US$14.99.
