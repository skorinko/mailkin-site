Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Mailkin is a Mac app that translates emails inside Mail. It is built so that your emails and your data stay on your Mac.

In short

  • Mailkin does not collect your emails, your name or anything you type, and never shares data with advertisers.
  • Your emails are translated on your Mac and are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • There is no account and no advertising. The mailkin.app version sends an anonymous "app opened" signal at most once a day, which you can turn off. The App Store version sends nothing.
  • mailkin.app counts visits without cookies, to learn which links bring visitors.
  • If you buy on mailkin.app, Paddle processes the payment and we receive your name and email to send your license key.

Your emails

To translate an email, Mail gives Mailkin's extension the email's content while you view it. Mailkin translates it on your Mac using Apple's built-in translation, which runs on the device, and hands the translated version back to Mail to show you. The email in your mailbox is not changed.

Mailkin keeps a local cache of recent translations on your Mac so emails open quickly the next time. It stays on your Mac and is never uploaded.

Data stored on your Mac

Mailkin stores its settings (your languages and per-sender choices), the translation cache and the count of free translations used on your Mac, in its own app container and in your Mac's keychain. None of it leaves your Mac, and none of it is sent to us.

Language downloads

Translation languages are downloaded by macOS from Apple. That download is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; Mailkin receives no information from it.

Purchases on the Mac App Store

A purchase in the App Store version is handled entirely by Apple. Mailkin only learns whether the purchase is active, so it can unlock unlimited translation. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account.

Purchases on mailkin.app

Purchases on this website are processed by Paddle.com, our reseller and Merchant of Record, under Paddle's Privacy Policy. Paddle collects your payment details; we never see them. Paddle shares with us your name, email address, country and order details, which we use only to send your license key, provide support, handle refunds and meet tax and legal obligations. We keep order records for as long as the law requires.

Your license key contains the email address you bought with. When you enter it, Mailkin checks it on your Mac and stores it in your Mac's keychain. The key is not sent to us when you use the app.

Update checks (mailkin.app version)

The version downloaded from mailkin.app checks for updates by downloading a list of releases from mailkin.app, like loading a web page. The request includes your IP address and the app's version, as any web request does. It doesn't include your emails, settings or license. You can turn automatic checks off in the update prompt. The App Store version is updated by the App Store.

Anonymous usage statistics (mailkin.app version)

So we know how many people use Mailkin, the version downloaded from mailkin.app sends a short message to mailkin.app when it is first opened, and at most once a day after that. It contains a random ID created on your Mac (not linked to your name, email or license), the app and macOS versions, and whether the app is unlocked. It never contains your emails, settings or anything you type. Your approximate location (country and city) is taken from the connection; your IP address is not stored.

To tell which website or ad led to a download, mailkin.app keeps, for up to 48 hours, a code derived from the downloading network's IP address with a secret that changes every day, together with the link the download came from. If the app's first message comes from the same network, it is credited to that link. The code can't be turned back into an IP address and is deleted after 48 hours.

You can turn this off in Mailkin's settings with "Share anonymous usage statistics". The App Store version sends no usage statistics.

This website

mailkin.app uses no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. To learn which links bring visitors, it records the pages you view, where you came from (the referring site and campaign tags in the link) and clicks on the download, pricing and buy buttons. These statistics are processed for us by PostHog in the EU. Your IP address is used to work out your approximate location and to count unique visitors per day, and is not kept in the statistics. The site runs through Cloudflare, which protects it and may process your IP address under Cloudflare's Privacy Policy. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security under GitHub's Privacy Statement. When you start a checkout, Paddle's checkout runs on this site and may set its own cookies.

Contacting us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to answer you. Questions about this policy: macapps.support@icloud.com.
