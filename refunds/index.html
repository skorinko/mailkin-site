Refund Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Bought on mailkin.app

If Mailkin isn't right for you, you can get a full refund within 14 days of your purchase, for any reason.

To ask for one, email macapps.support@icloud.com with the email address you used to buy, or reply to your receipt email. You can also contact Paddle, our reseller and Merchant of Record, through paddle.net. Refunds go back to your original payment method. Your bank may take a few days to show it.

After a refund, the license key from that purchase is deactivated and Mailkin goes back to the free allowance.

After 14 days, purchases are generally not refundable, but write to us if something went wrong and we will look at it. This doesn't affect your statutory rights as a consumer.

Bought on the Mac App Store

Apple handles payments and refunds for App Store purchases. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
